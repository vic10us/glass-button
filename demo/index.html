fire-glass-button

A real <button> under a WebGL2 render of thick glass with procedural fire inside. Hover, press, tab to it. Everything below is the same component at different sizes and settings.

Take Action →

Status modes

The status attribute recolours the fire, the glass rim and the floor glow, shows a matching icon and nudges a few parameters. Changing it crossfades the palette. icon="none" hides the icon; slot="icon" supplies your own.

States

Idle burns low. Hovering raises the flames, the lower glass illumination and the rim brightness over a few hundred milliseconds. Moving the pointer slides the key light across the glass. Pressing compresses the pill slightly and pulses the fire, then relaxes with no bounce.

Sizes

The shader works in units of pill height, so rims, refraction and flame scale stay proportional. The fire buffer keeps a fixed texel density per pill height instead of stretching.

Mobile width

An explicit width on the host stretches the pill; the fire still scales with the height.

Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the fire is frozen in place and the renderer only draws while a hover or press state is easing. The design stays intact. The toggle below forces it via the reducedMotion property.

Take Action →

CSS fallback

When WebGL2 is unavailable the element sets data-renderer="css" and the shadow stylesheet paints a simplified glass and glow. Setting that attribute yourself forces the fallback, as here.
